nastya.shchaveleva@internet.ru. The in-app feedback button adds the version details to the draft for you.

No sound comes out

A

Turn the ring/silent switch to ring and raise the volume.

B

Disconnect Bluetooth or wired audio: the tone must come from the phone itself.

C

Wipe the grille, hold the phone speaker-down over a cloth, try again.

Pro is not unlocked

A

Tap Restore on the paywall.

B

Use the Apple ID that paid.

C

Still locked? Email with the purchase date.

The score did not change

Quiet room, phone flat, same spot and same holding position for every check. A small gain still counts: water leaves in stages, so run a second check an hour later.

Microphone says denied

iPhone Settings, Spurto, Microphone, switch on. The eject tone works without it, but the score and the mic test need it.

Clear my history

Open History and tap the cross beside an entry. Deleting the app clears all of it.

Stop the subscription

iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Spurto, Cancel. Do it a day or more ahead of the trial end or renewal.

Phone is still muffled

Let it dry face-down for a few hours. If sound stays dull, the cause may not be water: check for lint in the grille or ask a repair shop.
