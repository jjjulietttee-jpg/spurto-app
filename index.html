What Spurto is, and where the paperwork lives.

What the app does

Spurto plays a 165 Hz tone through the iPhone speaker to shake out water, while a live grille animation shows the work. Before and after, it listens through the microphone and gives a 0 to 100 loudness score so you can see whether the speaker got clearer. Extras: left/right channel test, a frequency sweep, a microphone check and a vibration test.
