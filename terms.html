What Spurto does — and doesn't

Spurto plays a low 165 Hz tone through your iPhone's built-in speaker that can help push water out of the speaker grille, and measures loudness with the microphone. It does not repair hardware, does not remove dust, and does not replace drying your phone. If your phone was submerged, follow Apple's guidance and contact Apple Support. The tone stops by itself after 30 seconds; Spurto never changes your volume.
