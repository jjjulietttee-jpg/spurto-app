Please read this bit first

Sound can help water leave a speaker grille, but it is not magic. Spurto cannot promise that your phone will be dry, repaired or safe to charge. If liquid got in through more than the grille, or the phone shows a liquid alert, power it down and consult Apple or a repair shop.

Using the tone safely

  • Keep the iPhone away from your ears and from children while a tone plays.
  • A full eject plays two 30-second bursts with a 5-second rest and then stops by itself. Please do not restart it over and over.
  • Do not plug in a charger or headphones while the speaker is wet.
  • Stop if the speaker crackles worse or you feel warmth.

The readings

The 0 to 100 score compares loudness at a few tones picked up by your own microphone, in your room. It is a rough guide, not a lab instrument, and it moves with background noise and how you hold the phone.
