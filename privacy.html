Privacy, short and wet-proof

Last revised October 6, 2026.

Headline

Spurto has no account. When it listens, the sound is analysed on the phone and thrown away. Only a few numbers and a one-line verdict are saved, and they stay on the iPhone.

What we touch, and what we never do

ThingWhat happens
MicrophoneListens during an eject check and the mic test to measure loudness at a few probe tones. No recording is written to storage and no audio is uploaded.
Check historyFor each check the app saves the time, the type (eject, stereo, sweep, mic, vibration), a short verdict, and the before and after level readings. It lives in the app's own database on your device.
Quiz answersYour onboarding replies (what the speaker sounds like, when it got wet) and the first before and after readings sit in the phone's local settings store.
Contacts, photos, location, HealthNever requested.
Ads and trackersNone. No advertising identifier, no cross-app tracking.

The outside party

Subscription status goes through Adapty. It gets a random profile ID, the purchase and renewal events, basic device and app-version details and how you interact with the paywall. It never gets your history or any sound. Apple handles the actual payment.

Writing to us

The feedback button opens a draft email with a footer listing app version, iOS release and an Adapty profile number, which lets us locate a purchase. You can edit or delete it before sending.

Wiping it

In the History tab, tap the cross on any entry to delete that check. Deleting the Spurto app clears the history, quiz answers and everything else stored on the phone. There is no copy on a server to ask us about; to have the Adapty profile removed, email us the ID from a feedback draft.

Permissions

Allow or refuse the microphone in iPhone Settings, Spurto. Without it, the eject still plays but the score and the mic test cannot run.

Backups, kids, changes

If you back up your iPhone, Apple may include the app's data in that backup. Spurto is a utility not aimed at young children and gathers nothing about their age. A revised version of this page keeps this address and carries a new date. Questions: nastya.shchaveleva@internet.ru.
