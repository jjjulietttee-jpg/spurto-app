Privacy Policy

Spurto: Water Eject & Cleaner · updated October 3, 2026

Spurto is made by Anastassiya Shugaley. Short version: sound and measurements stay on your iPhone.

Microphone

With your permission, Spurto listens through the microphone for about two seconds before and after an eject to measure how loud your speaker sounds, during the frequency sweep, and in the microphone test. The audio is analysed live on the iPhone. It is never uploaded and never stored, except the 5-second microphone test clip, which is kept in memory only until you leave that screen.

What stays on your device

  • Your check history (date, test type, loudness numbers and verdict).
  • Your two onboarding answers (what happened, when) and settings.

Spurto does not use the camera, location, contacts or photos. It has no accounts.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. To manage subscriptions and measure how the paywall performs, Spurto uses Adapty as a processor. Adapty receives an anonymous app-generated profile ID, purchase and subscription status, device model, OS version, app version, country/region derived from the IP address, which onboarding screen was reached and the two onboarding answers. Spurto does not collect your name, email or advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Spurto is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting data

Delete a check with ✕ in History, or delete the app to remove everything on the device. For data held by Adapty for your anonymous profile, write to nastya.shchaveleva@internet.ru with the profile ID from the feedback email.
